### 3.1 Classifier-Free Guidance Methods
Description: This subsection explores various classifier-free guidance methods for aligning diffusion models, including their applications, advantages, and limitations. Classifier-free guidance has become a fundamental tool in modern diffusion models for text-guided generation, offering a way to control the generation process without explicit classification.
1. CFG++: An enhanced classifier-free guidance approach that tackles the off-manifold challenges inherent in traditional CFG, offering significant improvements in sample quality for text-to-image generation.
2. Adaptive Projected Guidance (APG): A method that retains the quality-boosting advantages of CFG while enabling the use of higher guidance scales without oversaturation, making it a superior plug-and-play alternative to standard classifier-free guidance.
3. Model-Guidance (MG): A novel objective for training diffusion models that addresses and removes the need for classifier-free guidance, resulting in accelerated training and exceptional quality that parallels or surpasses concurrent diffusion models with CFG.

### 3.2 Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback
Description: This subsection delves into the application of reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF) for aligning diffusion models with human preferences and values. RLHF involves training a reward model on human feedback and using this model to fine-tune the diffusion model, aiming to improve alignment with human preferences.
1. RLHF with Preference Models: A method that assumes a model of human preferences to approximate the human's unobservable reward function, with interventions such as showing humans the quantities underlying a preference model to improve preference data quality.
2. Policy-Guided DPO (PG-DPO): A framework that combines Adaptive Rejection Scaling (ARS) and Implicit Preference Regularization (IPR) to effectively mitigate likelihood displacement in diffusion models, leading to improved preference alignment.
3. HERO: A framework that leverages online human feedback collected during model learning, featuring Feedback-Aligned Representation Learning and Feedback-Guided Image Generation to efficiently utilize human feedback for model fine-tuning.

### 3.3 Preference-Based Optimization Methods
Description: This subsection focuses on preference-based optimization methods designed to incorporate user preferences into the generation process of diffusion models. These methods aim to optimize the model's parameters to better align with human preferences, often using preference data or reward functions.
1. Direct Preference Optimization (DPO): A method that optimizes the diffusion model to align with human preferences by directly using preference data, without the need for a reward model or explicit human feedback.
2. Divergence Minimization Preference Optimization (DMPO): A novel method that aligns diffusion models by minimizing reverse KL divergence, providing a principled and effective approach to preference alignment.
3. Tailored Preference Optimization (TailorPO): A framework that directly ranks intermediate noisy samples based on their step-wise reward, effectively resolving gradient direction issues and enhancing optimization effectiveness in preference alignment.

### 3.4 Inference-Time Alignment Control
Description: This subsection explores methods for controlling the alignment of diffusion models at inference time, allowing for dynamic adjustment of the model's output to better match desired preferences or criteria without requiring additional training.
1. Reinforcement Learning Guidance (RLG): An inference-time method that adapts Classifier-Free Guidance (CFG) by combining the outputs of the base and RL fine-tuned models via a geometric average, enabling dynamic control over the alignment-quality trade-off.
2. Null-Text Test-Time Alignment (Null-TTA): A method that aligns diffusion models by optimizing the unconditional embedding in classifier-free guidance, ensuring alignment occurs on a semantically coherent manifold and prevents reward hacking.
3. Training-Free Constrained Generation: A approach that integrates stable diffusion models with constrained optimization frameworks, enabling the generation of outputs that satisfy stringent physical and functional requirements without additional training.
