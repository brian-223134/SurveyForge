1. **Supervised Fine-Tuning**: Utilizes labeled datasets to fine-tune models, enhancing their ability to follow instructions by learning from explicit feedback.
2. **Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback**: Leverages human feedback to train models, allowing them to learn from implicit cues and improve their instruction-following capabilities.
3. **Direct Preference Optimization**: Focuses on directly optimizing model preferences to align with human values and instructions, ensuring more ethical and responsible AI behavior.

### 3.2 Parameter-Efficient Tuning Methods
Description: This subsection delves into parameter-efficient tuning methods, such as adapters, prefix tuning, and low-rank adaptations, which reduce the computational requirements and memory usage of instruction tuning without compromising model performance.
1. **Adapters**: Small neural networks inserted into pre-trained models to adapt them to new tasks with minimal additional parameters.
2. **Prefix Tuning**: Involves tuning a small set of prefix vectors in the model to adapt to new instructions, reducing the need for full model fine-tuning.
3. **Low-Rank Adaptations**: Methods that update model weights in a low-rank subspace, efficiently adapting the model to new tasks while preserving its pre-trained knowledge.
